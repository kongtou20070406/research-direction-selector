# Prospective structure-exploration evaluation

Real model-matched trials: **NOT_RUN**. Scientific benefit: **UNMEASURED**.
The scripted harness validates the protocol; its prescribed baseline cannot
estimate an unaided LLM's ability.

Freeze before execution: task/grader hashes, unused seeds, model/version/effort/
speed, prompts, tool/source visibility, initial goal/data/acceptance/graph,
total token/currency/CPU/GPU/wall caps, trial count and campaign cap.
Extra paid calls or private-data transfer require explicit authorization.
Negative outcomes never reset the original budget.

| Arm | Intervention |
| --- | --- |
| No graph suggestions | Same model/tools/evidence/total budget; plain goal/evidence prompt. |
| Edges only | Same initial model; adds evidence/edges without concepts or replacing decomposition. |
| Expansion and reconstruction | Same initial model; open task packets, new concepts and reversible alternative structures. |

Use all three public task families plus unused harder cases where unaided models
actually fail. Give no arm the correct bridge initially. Proposal prompts see
no grader answers, held-out outcomes or future information. All arms share the
same primary-source retrieval and independent verification tools.

Count retrieval, proposals, planning, execution, failures, independent checks,
replanning and optional training. Report measured values, estimates and UNKNOWN
dimensions separately. Exhaustion, timeout, malformed output, uncertain delivery
and human rescue remain outcomes, not excluded trials.

Primary endpoint: independently verified original-task success with denominators
and every failure. Also report time from first settled dead-end evidence to an
independently validated viable route, total cost per resource unit, and human
interventions/reasons. Preserve trajectories linking suggestions to runs, original
results and subsequent decisions, including unused suggestions and retractions.
Never use suggestion/node counts, complexity or new wording as benefit.

Small samples support scoped descriptions only. Report uncertainty and task/model
limits. Formal preservation, mechanism evidence, recovery and research-policy gain
remain distinct. A future learner needs states/actions/observations/costs, reliable
probabilities or an explicit heuristic label, unused validation and training cost.
